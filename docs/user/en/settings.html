Settings and privacy

Right-click the tray icon and open Global settings to change double-click behavior, exclude apps from capture or move your local history.

On this page
  • Set double-click to Copy only
  • Choose when the first card updates the clipboard
  • Exclude an app from clipboard capture
  • Find or move your history folder
  • Customize shortcuts and appearance

Set double-click to Copy only

Open Global settings → Cards → Hanging double-click. The default, Copy and paste, sends a paste to the original destination if it can still accept one. Choose Copy only to restore the contents to the clipboard, move the card to the front of its rope and hide the workspace. Click where you want the contents and press Ctrl+V.

On the same page, Hanging card deletion and Floating card deletion control what × does in each form. See which closed cards you can recover from ReBin.

Choose when the first card updates the clipboard

Ropes → Update clipboard when switching ropes
On by default. Switching to a nonempty collecting rope copies its first card to the clipboard for your next manual paste.
Cards → Update clipboard when the first card changes
On by default. When you rearrange the current collecting rope and a different card becomes first, its contents are copied. Saving an edit to the first card’s text also updates the clipboard.

Scrolling through cards, canceling a drag or switching to an empty rope leaves the clipboard as it was. Neither setting sends a paste shortcut to another window.

Exclude an app from clipboard capture

  1. Open Storage & Privacy → Clipboard capture exclusions and choose Edit list….
  2. Enter the app’s executable name, such as KeePass.exe, and choose Add. Enter the filename without its folder path.
  3. Choose Save. Future copies from the listed apps are skipped. Cards already in your history stay there.

Find or move your history folder

The default data folder is %LOCALAPPDATA%\ClipRope. Open Storage & Privacy to see the active Data folder and its Disk usage. ClipRope stores history and Floating card positions on this PC. Referenced files remain in their original locations.

Choose Browse… beside Data folder and select an empty folder. Choose Restart now to start the move, or Later to keep using the current folder until the next launch. Use Cancel move to cancel a pending migration.

Customize shortcuts and appearance

On the Shortcuts page, select an action, record a key combination and choose Check to see whether it is available. If another app already uses the workspace shortcut, you can still open ClipRope from the tray. See default shortcuts and where they work.

Under Appearance → Theme, select Light, Dark or System. Find highlight color marks the current match on a card. The theme button at the top of this guide changes the guide’s appearance only.

Organizing ropes

Keep separate histories for different tasks. New copies go to the current collecting rope. Each ordinary rope has its own card order and capacity.

On this page
  • Create or switch collecting ropes
  • Browse and reorder ropes in overview
  • Move or copy a card
  • Adjust or clear a rope from its menu
  • What happens when a rope is full?

Create or switch collecting ropes

Right-click a rope line or its wooden nameplate and choose Add new rope…. Enter a nonblank name (up to 256 UTF-16 units; some characters count as two), choose a color and enter a positive whole number for capacity. Save, then choose Yes when asked whether to switch. The next item you copy goes to the new rope.

With the pointer over a visible rope row or the workspace focused, press Tab to select the next collecting rope. In single-rope view, you can also hold Ctrl and scroll up for the previous rope or down for the next. By default, switching to a nonempty rope copies its first card to the clipboard. Change when the clipboard follows the first card.

Browse and reorder ropes in overview

Point at a rope row and press Ctrl+Tab to switch between single-rope view and overview. In overview, double-click a rope line or nameplate to make it the collecting rope and open its single-rope view.

Move or copy a card

To move a card, drag the middle of its paper over the target rope. Release when the neighboring cards leave a gap. To reach a position offscreen, hold the pointer at either end of the row and let it scroll. Press Esc to cancel.

To copy, hold Ctrl before dragging the paper. The view at the start of the drag determines the destination:

Overview: copy onto a rope
Drag a Hanging or Floating card into a gap on any visible rope. A Floating original stays on the desktop. Release outside all rows to cancel. The destination’s capacity limit still applies.
Single-rope view or workspace hidden: copy to another app
Drag a Hanging card from single-rope view to an app that accepts its contents. A Floating card can do the same with the single rope visible or the workspace hidden. Drop into a File Explorer folder to copy a file or save a screenshot as an image. To duplicate a card on the same rope, open overview first.

During a drag to another app, press the right mouse button or Esc before releasing the left button to cancel the drop.

Adjust or clear a rope from its menu

Right-click the line or nameplate of the rope you want. In overview, the menu acts on the row you clicked.

Edit style… · Card density
Change the name, color or capacity inline. Use − / + or type an exact capacity. Review the affected tail cards before confirming a decrease. Compact or Regular density is remembered separately for each rope.
Back to first card · Previous rope / Next rope
Back to first card scrolls to the head without copying it. Previous and Next switch the collecting rope.
Rope overview · Find in Cards · Show / hide ReBin
Switch between All ropes and This rope, search the Hanging history, or recover cards from ReBin.
Add new rope… · Clear Hanging cards… · Delete this rope…
Clearing keeps the rope; deleting removes it as well. Both show the number of affected Hanging cards before you confirm and leave Floating cards alone. Neither main nor the system ReBin can be renamed or deleted. ReBin always has unlimited capacity.

What happens when a rope is full?

Each ordinary rope has its own limit. Card 1 is at the front and the last card is at the tail. Copying a new item or clicking Return adds a card at the front. If this puts the rope over capacity, it loses its last card.

C was last, so it drops off. Cards on other ropes keep their positions. If the return cannot be saved, A, B and C stay in their original order and D stays on the desktop.

To change an existing rope’s limit, right-click its line or nameplate and choose Edit style…. ClipRope asks for confirmation before reducing capacity. Global settings → Ropes → Capacity for new ropes applies to ropes you create later.
